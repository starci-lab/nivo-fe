Academy entry — three directions

The landing already runs in production. The only page with no incumbent is the one a student enters through, so that is where the choice is. All three directions keep the same landing and the same academy chrome; they differ in where entry happens.

What the evidence fixes, and what it leaves open

Fixed. OAuth leads. auth.e2e-spec.ts:231 calls exchangeOauthCode “the login”, and the registry already encodes the order: auth-entry-stack declares OAuth shortcuts closed by an OR divider above, the credential form below. Both lanes create the member; a refusal writes nothing.

Fixed. There is no signUp, no password reset and no 2FA on the academy API. Any direction promising them is describing the control plane, not this product.

Open. The backend built the password grant “so the FE can log a member in from a modal, with no browser redirect”. But OAuth genuinely leaves the browser, and a signed-in student has to be sent somewhere. Page or modal is therefore a real choice.

Entry is its own destination. A route the student can be sent to, linked to, and returned to.

E-AB · SELECTED — one block, two hosts

The chosen shape is not E-A or E-C but a hybrid of E-A and E-B, and it is the shape starci-academy-fe already ships: src/app/authentication, src/components/overlays/auth/SignInOverlay and src/components/shells/ModalShell.

Retained from E-A: a real address at /[locale]/sign-in — the destination a guard redirects to, the page a link opens, and the address OAuth returns to. Retained from E-B: a quick-access overlay so a reader on the landing can enter without losing the page. Rejected from E-B: the claim that entry needs no address — OAuth leaves the browser, so that lane hands off to the route rather than pretending to stay. Rejected from E-C: muting the landing behind a route; the overlay covers that need without asking whether centred-authentication-page's “only task” still holds.
